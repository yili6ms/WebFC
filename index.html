On touch screens, use the on-screen controller; slide between buttons, or press between A and B to hold both. Add WebFC to your home screen for a full-screen app.

About

WebFC includes free, openly licensed homebrew games under Games (licenses). Your own ROMs are read locally and never leave your device. Supported boards: iNES mapper 0 (NROM), 2 (UxROM) and 3 (CNROM), NTSC. Audio and save states are not yet implemented by the core.
